# Monetization model

## Free tier

- VPN connectivity remains usable without payment.
- Ads are displayed inside TunnelPilot only.
- No proxying or modification of other applications' traffic for ad monetization.

## Rewarded ads

Potential reward: a temporary in-app benefit such as a premium preference or temporary server priority. The ad must be explicitly opted into by the user.

## Premium

Potential benefits:

- no ads
- larger server catalog
- higher server priority
- optional speed/capacity tier

The VPN data path never depends on an ad impression.
